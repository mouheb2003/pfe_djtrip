# DJTrip FAQ & Step-by-Step Guides

This document contains the most common questions asked by Tourists and Organizers, along with **highly detailed, step-by-step guides** on how to perform each action in the app.

---

## 🏖️ Guides for Tourists

### 1. How do I find activities happening around me today?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Open the DJTrip app and ensure you are logged in as a **Tourist**.
2. Tap on the **Explore** tab (the compass icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
3. At the top of the screen, you will see a search bar. You can type specific keywords here.
4. Below the search bar, swipe through the **Categories** (Adventure, Culture, Relax, etc.) and tap one to filter the results.
5. Alternatively, tap the **Map Icon** to view the interactive map. The map will show your current location with pins indicating nearby activities. Tap any pin to see a preview of the activity.

### 2. How do I book a spot for an activity?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Navigate to the **Explore** tab and find an activity you like.
2. Tap on the activity card to open the **Activity Detail Screen**.
3. Scroll down to review the description, date, price, and remaining spots (`places disponibles`).
4. At the very bottom of the screen, tap the prominent **"Book Now"** (or "Réserver") button.
5. A confirmation popup will appear. Confirm your booking. 
6. You will receive a success notification, and the activity will now appear in your "My Activities" tab.

### 3. Where can I see the activities I have booked?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Tap the **My Activities** tab (the calendar/ticket icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
2. At the top of the screen, you will see two tabs: **Upcoming** and **Past**.
3. Tap **Upcoming** to see all future activities you are registered for.
4. Tap **Past** to see activities that have already concluded.

### 4. Can I cancel a booking?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **My Activities** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Ensure you are on the **Upcoming** section.
3. Find the activity you wish to cancel and tap on it to open the details.
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap the **Cancel Booking** button (often displayed in red).
5. Confirm your cancellation in the popup dialog.

### 5. How do I contact an Organizer before booking?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Open the **Activity Detail Screen** of the activity you are interested in.
2. Scroll down to the "Organizer Info" section.
3. Tap on the Organizer's name or avatar photo to open their **Public Profile**.
4. On their profile page, look for the **Message** or **Chat** icon button (usually near the top right or under their bio).
5. Tap it to open the private **Chat Conversation Screen** and type your question.

### 6. How do I leave a review after an activity?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **My Activities** tab and select the **Past** section.
2. Tap on the activity you recently completed.
3. On the details page, tap the **Leave a review** button.
4. A popup will appear. Tap the stars (1 to 5) to rate the experience.
5. Type your feedback in the text box and tap **Submit**. This rating will appear on the Organizer's public profile.

### 7. How do I share my trip photos with others?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Tap the **Social** tab (the grid/feed icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the floating **'+' (Create Post)** button, usually located at the bottom right of the screen.
3. Select a photo from your gallery or take a new one using your camera.
4. Write a caption or description for your post.
5. Tap **Publish** or **Post** at the top right. Your post will now be visible to your followers and on the global feed.

### 8. How can I change my profile picture (avatar) or cover photo?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Tap the **Profile** tab (the person icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon located in the top right corner of the screen.
3. Select **Edit Profile** from the list of options.
4. Tap the camera icon overlaying your current avatar or cover photo.
5. Choose a new image from your gallery.
6. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Save** to apply the changes.

### 9. Why is the map not showing any markers?
**Step-by-step guide to fix:**
1. Check your phone's settings to ensure DJTrip has permission to access your **Location**.
2. If location is enabled, go to the **Explore** tab and verify that you haven't selected a highly restrictive category filter. Tap the "All" category to clear filters.
3. Try zooming out on the map using a pinch gesture, as there might not be activities within a 1km radius of your exact current location.

### 10. How do I turn off push notifications?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Navigate to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the top right corner.
3. Tap on **Notifications** or **Privacy Settings**.
4. You will see toggle switches for different types of alerts (New Messages, Bookings, System).
5. Tap the switches to turn them off (they will turn grey). Changes are saved automatically.

### 11. Can I follow my favorite organizers?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Whenever you are on an Organizer's **Public Profile** (accessed via an activity or the search bar).
2. Look for the prominent **Follow** button under their profile picture and bio.
3. Tap it. The button will change to "Following". Their future posts will now be prioritized in your Social tab feed.

### 12. How do I change the app language?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon.
3. Scroll down and tap on **Language**.
4. Select your preferred language from the list (e.g., English, French, Arabic). The app will immediately reload in the chosen language.

### 13. Is it free to use the DJTrip app?
**Answer:**
Yes! Creating a Tourist account, browsing activities, messaging organizers, and using the social feed are 100% free. You only pay for the specific activities you actively choose to book (if they have a price listed).

### 14. What do I do if an organizer doesn't show up?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. First, check your **Chat** tab to ensure the organizer didn't send a last-minute emergency message.
2. If they are completely unreachable, go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Settings**, then scroll down to **Help & Support**.
4. Tap **Contact Us** or **Report an Issue** and provide the Activity ID and Organizer name so admins can investigate.

### 15. How can I view an image in full screen?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to any user's profile (your own in the **Profile** tab, or someone else's **Public Profile**).
2. Tap directly on their circular **Avatar** or their rectangular **Cover Photo**.
3. The image will instantly expand to fill the entire screen with a blurred background.
4. To exit, simply tap anywhere on the screen or tap the 'X' icon.

## 📅 Guides for Organizers

### 16. How do I create a new activity?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Log in as an Organizer and tap the **Manage Activities** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the large **'+' (Create Activity)** button or floating action button.
3. **Step 1:** Enter the Title, Description, and select a Category.
4. **Step 2:** Choose the Start Date, End Date, and Time.
5. **Step 3:** Enter the Price and the maximum number of available spots (`Capacity`).
6. **Step 4:** Pick a location on the interactive map.
7. **Step 5:** Upload a thumbnail and additional photos for the gallery.
8. Tap **Publish Activity**. It will now appear on the Tourists' Explore feed.

### 17. How can I see who booked my activity?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Manage Activities** tab.
2. Locate the specific activity in your "Active" or "Upcoming" list.
3. Tap on the activity card.
4. Scroll down to the **Participants** section. Here you will see a list of all Tourists who have successfully booked a spot, along with their avatars and names.

### 18. Can I edit an activity after creating it?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Manage Activities** tab and tap on the activity you wish to edit.
2. Look for the **Edit** icon (usually a pencil icon) in the top right corner.
3. Modify the necessary fields (e.g., lower the price or fix a typo in the description).
4. Tap **Save Changes** at the bottom. *(Note: You cannot change critical details like date/time if users have already booked).*

### 19. How do I set my location on the map for an activity?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. While in the **Create Activity** or **Edit Activity** screen, reach the Location section.
2. A map view will appear. You can pan and zoom the map with your fingers.
3. Tap and hold on the exact spot where the activity starts to drop a red pin.
4. Ensure the coordinates update correctly in the text fields below the map, then proceed to the next step.

### 20. Why isn't my activity showing up on the Tourist Explore feed?
**Step-by-step troubleshooting:**
1. Go to **Manage Activities** and open the activity.
2. Check the **Date**: Ensure the activity date is in the future.
3. Check the **Capacity**: Ensure `places_disponibles` is greater than 0. If it is 0, the activity is considered "Sold Out" and hidden from the main feed.
4. Check the **Status**: Ensure you haven't archived or deleted the activity.

### 21. How do I get the 'Verified Organizer' badge?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Ensure your profile is 100% complete (Avatar, Cover Photo, Bio, Specialties).
2. Successfully host at least 3 activities with positive ratings.
3. Go to **Profile > Settings > Help & Support** and send a message to the administrators requesting verification. They will review your profile and grant the blue checkmark badge.

### 22. Can I cancel an activity if I have an emergency?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Manage Activities** tab.
2. Tap the activity you need to cancel.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Cancel Activity**.
4. Confirm the cancellation. **Important:** The system will automatically send a notification to all registered tourists informing them of the cancellation.

### 23. How do I reply to a Tourist's message?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. You will receive a push notification when a tourist messages you.
2. Tap the **Chat** or **Inbox** icon (usually located at the top right of the main screen).
3. Find the conversation thread with the tourist's name and tap it.
4. Type your response in the text box at the bottom and tap the send icon (paper plane).

### 24. How are my average ratings calculated?
**Answer:**
Your global rating (e.g., 4.8/5) is calculated automatically by the server. It takes the average of all the 1 to 5-star reviews left by tourists across *all* the past activities you have hosted. You cannot delete negative reviews yourself, but you can report abusive reviews to the admins.

### 25. How can I attract more tourists to my activities?
**Step-by-step tips:**
1. **Optimize your Profile:** Go to the Profile tab and add high-quality Cover/Avatar photos. Fill out a detailed bio.
2. **Post on Social:** Go to the Social tab and post engaging photos of your past events.
3. **Competitive Pricing:** Ensure your prices are fair compared to similar activities in the Explore tab.
4. **Great Photos:** When creating an activity, upload bright, clear, and attractive images.

### 26. Can another organizer review my profile?
**Answer:**
No. To prevent review manipulation and unfair competition, the app logic strictly hides the "Leave a review" button when an Organizer is viewing another Organizer's profile. Only Tourists who have participated in an activity can leave reviews.

### 27. How do I add my spoken languages and specialties?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon.
3. Select **Edit Profile**.
4. Scroll down to the **Languages** and **Specialties** input fields.
5. Type your skills separated by commas (e.g., "English, French, Italian" and "Scuba Diving, History").
6. Tap **Save**. These will now appear as tags on your public profile.

### 28. Is there a limit to how many activities I can create?
**Answer:**
No, your account does not have a hard limit on activity creation. However, the system monitors organizer performance. If you create dozens of activities but constantly cancel them or receive 1-star reviews, your account may be flagged by administrators.

### 29. What happens if an activity is fully booked?
**Answer:**
The system handles this automatically. When the number of reservations matches your defined capacity (`places_disponibles` becomes 0), the "Book Now" button on the activity details page will automatically turn gray and say "Sold Out" or "Full". No further bookings can be made unless a registered tourist cancels their spot.

### 30. How can I delete my account?
**Step-by-step guide:**
1. Go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear icon.
3. Scroll down and tap on **Privacy & Security**.
4. At the very bottom, tap **Delete Account**.
5. You will be prompted to enter your password to confirm. **Warning:** This action is irreversible and will delete all your activities, posts, and data.
